# Thumbnails and SEO

Output: `episodes/EP###/metadata/` with `youtube.md`, `tiktok.md`, `instagram.md`, `thumbnail_a.png`, `thumbnail_b.png`.

## Who searches

Kids search with simple words ("ninja", "karate", "taekwondo for kids", "tiger cartoon"), often by voice. Parents search for value ("taekwondo for kids at home", "kids self defense lesson", "taekwondo belt meaning", "chon-ji pattern for kids"). Write for both.

## Titles (YouTube)

- 40-60 characters, the hook first. Pattern: `<Exciting hook> | <what they learn>`.
  - "The Tiger in My Belt! | White Belt Meaning for Kids"
  - "The Door of Heaven and Earth | Chon-Ji Pattern Story"
- Include the pattern or belt name in every episode title (it's what parents search).
- No ALL CAPS sentences, no misleading claims, no "you won't believe".

## Thumbnails

- 1280x720, readable on a phone at 2 cm wide.
- One big character face with clear emotion (Kai or Bori), one bold object (glowing door, belt), at most 3 words of text in the brand font.
- Belt colour of the episode as the dominant accent (consistency helps kids spot the series).
- Must show something that actually happens in the video.
- Make 2 variants; use YouTube's thumbnail test feature where available and log winners in `analytics/`.

## Descriptions (YouTube)

```

In this episode:

```

Chapters need at least 3, starting at 00:00. Do not put external links aimed at kids.

## Tags and hashtags

- YouTube tags (low weight but useful for misspellings): taekwondo for kids, ITF taekwondo, taekwon-do, <pattern name>, <belt> belt meaning, kids martial arts cartoon, self defense for kids, anime for kids.
- TikTok/Instagram: 3-5 hashtags aimed at parents: #taekwondo #taekwondokids #kidsactivities #parentingtips #martialarts. No trend-jacking hashtags unrelated to the content.

## Playlists

One playlist per season ("Season 1: White Belt"), plus "Safety Lessons", "Pattern Stories", and "Bori's Facts". Order the season playlist by episode for autoplay.

## Vertical captions (TikTok/Reels)

First line = hook for parents, second = what kids learn, third = "Full episode on YouTube: <channel name>". Choose a cover frame with a readable face and the belt colour.
