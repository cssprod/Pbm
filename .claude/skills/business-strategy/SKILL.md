# Business strategy

Not financial or legal advice; the owner makes the decisions. Output lives in `business/` (`plan.md`, `quarterly/`, `partners.md`).

## Mission and goals

Mission: help kids grow strong, kind, and brave through the stories and values of Taekwon-Do, and make something the owner's son loves.

Suggested milestone ladder (update in `business/plan.md`):

| Phase | Goal | Signal to move on |
|-------|------|-------------------|
| 0. Pilot | 1 main episode + 3 shorts; son loves it | Test viewer asks for more; cost per episode known |
| 1. Season 1 | 6-8 episodes, steady cadence | Avg % viewed > 50%, returning viewers growing |
| 2. Monetise | YouTube Partner Program eligibility | Meet YPP thresholds; first partner school |
| 3. Expand | Season 2+, partnerships, products | Revenue covers production cost |

## Revenue streams (and their limits)

1. **YouTube ads (YPP):** MFK means no personalised ads, so revenue per view is low. Treat it as a base, not the plan. Watch YouTube's policies on AI-generated, repetitive content to stay eligible.
2. **Taekwon-Do school partnerships (strongest fit):** schools license episodes to show in class, use clips for their marketing, or sponsor episodes (sponsor credit aimed at parents). Offer a "class kit" (episode + printable pattern sheet + safety lesson guide).
3. **Printables and books (to parents):** pattern colouring pages, belt-progress charts, a picture book per season. Sold via a parent-facing site, never linked to kids from MFK videos.
4. **Merch:** tiger/Bori plush, belt-coloured wristbands. Note the YouTube merch shelf is unavailable on MFK; sell via the parent site and partner schools.
5. **Licensing:** to kids' streaming platforms, schools, and Taekwon-Do organisations once a season exists.
6. **Sponsors:** family brands only, disclosed, aimed at parents (see `child-safety-compliance`).
7. **Grants:** arts, cultural-heritage, or educational media grants (Korean cultural organisations, local arts councils).

## Unit economics per episode

Keep `business/unit-economics.md`, updated monthly from `finance/ledger.csv`:

```
Cost per main episode = AI generation + voice + music + tools (share of subscriptions) + reviewer fee + owner hours x rate
Cost per short = ...
Revenue per episode (90 days) = ads + partner fees + attributed sales
Break-even views at current RPM = cost / (RPM / 1000)
```

Target: drive generation cost down with reuse (backgrounds, recurring sequences) and batching.

## Setup checklist (owner decides)

- Business entity (e.g. an LLC) and separate bank account before significant revenue or contracts.
- Business email, domain, and matching social handles.
- Simple contracts: voice actors, composer, instructor reviewer, partner schools.
- Insurance or legal check if offering self-defense instruction products.

## Partnership pitch (schools)

One page: who we are, sample episode link, what the school gets (class kit, co-branding on their channels, credit), what we ask (feedback, sharing, a modest fee or sponsorship). Track outreach in `business/partners.md`. The agent drafts emails; the owner sends them.

## Quarterly review

Each quarter write `business/quarterly/YYYY-Qn.md`: goals vs actuals, spend vs budget, what to stop/start/continue, next quarter goals. Present to the owner for decisions.
