# Sabum Han's Safety Lesson

Every episode ends with a 45-60 second lesson. It is the most copied part of the show, so it must be safe, accurate, and approved by a qualified instructor before release.

## Lesson library

Lessons live in `references/itf/self-defense-lessons.md`, numbered SD-01 onward. Use them in order unless the story needs a specific one. If a new lesson is needed, add it there with status `draft` and flag it for instructor review; never publish a `draft` lesson.

If the library file and the table below differ, the library file wins. Suggested progression (Season 1 to 2):

| # | Topic | Core skill |
|---|-------|-----------|
| SD-01 | Ready stance and safe distance | Stand ready, hands up "fence", one big step back |
| SD-02 | The strong voice | Loud "STOP! BACK AWAY!" with confident posture |
| SD-03 | Trusted adults | Who they are, how to find one in a store or park |
| SD-04 | Wrist grab escape | Rotate toward the thumb, pull away, run, tell |
| SD-05 | Safe falling (back breakfall) | Tuck chin, slap mat, protect head; on soft surface only |
| SD-06 | Bullying: walk, talk, tell | Walk away, firm words, tell an adult |
| SD-07 | Stranger safety and "tricky people" | Adults shouldn't ask kids for help; check first |
| SD-08 | Body boundaries | My body belongs to me; say no, tell |
| SD-09 | Lost in public | Stay put, find a safe helper, know a phone number |
| SD-10 | Two-hand front grab escape | Step back, break grip, distance, run, tell |
| SD-11 | Bear hug from behind (basic) | Drop weight, make noise, wriggle free, run, tell |
| SD-12 | Online safety basics | Don't share name/school; tell a grown-up if something feels weird |

## Required structure (every lesson)

1. **Situation (5-10 s):** a relatable, non-scary setup, ideally from the episode's story.
2. **The rule (5 s):** a short chant. E.g. "Stop, step back, speak up!"
3. **Demonstration (15-20 s):** Sabum Han shows it slowly from the front and side; on-screen arrows.
4. **Kids practise (10-15 s):** Kai/Hana/Leo try it; one gets it a bit wrong and is corrected kindly.
5. **Your turn (5-10 s):** "Practise with a grown-up, slowly."
6. **Safety reminder card (3 s, on screen and spoken):** "Always practise with a grown-up. The best self-defense is to get away and tell a trusted adult."

## Non-negotiable safety rules

- The goal is always **escape and tell**, never "win the fight".
- No strikes to the head, throat, eyes, groin, or joints are taught. No chokes, locks, or weapons.
- No techniques that need a partner to be hurt to work. Partner practice is slow, with a grown-up.
- Breakfalls and anything involving the floor: practise on a soft surface (mat, bed, grass), with a grown-up.
- Never imply a child is at fault for being grabbed or bullied.
- Body-safety and stranger topics use the "tricky people" framing, not "stranger danger" fear. Keep it calm and empowering, never frightening. These topics get extra review.
- Bullying lessons never advise hitting back. Taekwon-Do is used only to protect yourself when there is no other way, and the tenet of self-control is named.
- Use correct anatomy-neutral language appropriate for 6-10.

## Review gate

Before any episode with a new lesson is published, `qa-report.md` must record: the instructor who reviewed it (name, rank), the date, and any changes. The owner can add a qualified ITF instructor's name in `brand/brand.md` as the default reviewer. Without that sign-off, the episode stays at `stage: qa` with a blocker.

## On-screen disclaimer (description and end card)

"This video is for general education and fun. Practise only with a parent or qualified instructor. If you are ever in danger, get away and tell a trusted adult or call your local emergency number."
