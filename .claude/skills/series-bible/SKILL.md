# Series bible

**Working title:** *Tiger Belt: The 24 Doors* (working title only; run a trademark check before launch, see `rights-and-licensing`).

**Logline:** Eight-year-old Kai ties on his first white belt and discovers that every Taekwon-Do pattern opens a door into a living legend. With his friends and a tiny tiger spirit named Bori, he learns that the real power of a black belt is a brave, kind heart.

**Audience:** boys 6-10 (girls welcome). The owner's son is the test viewer; his reactions outrank our opinions.

## The world

- **The Dojang (real world):** a small, warm neighbourhood Taekwon-Do school. Wooden floor, Korean and local flags, the five tenets painted on the wall.
- **The Great Clock (magic world):** a giant sky-clock with 24 doors, one for each pattern (the 24 patterns stand for the 24 hours of a day). When a student performs a pattern with true focus, its door glows and they step into that pattern's legend.
- **The Belt Tree:** a tree behind the Clock that grows as Kai progresses: seed (white), sprout in earth (yellow), green leaves (green), reaching to the sky (blue), red blossoms (red), a mighty old tree that stands through storms (black). This visualises the real ITF belt meanings.

### Rules of the magic (do not break)
1. Doors open through focus, practice, and a tenet, never through strength alone.
2. In a legend, kids can watch, help, and learn; they never change real history.
3. The Gloom (below) is defeated by tenets, never by hurting anyone.
4. Every adventure brings back one lesson to the real-world problem Kai faces that episode.

## Tone

- Exciting first, wise second. Think adventure anime with heart, at a gentle level.
- Humor in every scene (Bori and Leo carry most of it).
- Conflict is real but small-scale for kids: a sibling fight, a lost game, a bully, being scared of a test.
- No sarcasm aimed at adults or parents. Adults are wise and kind, sometimes funny.
- Safe-combat rule: sparring is shown with gear, control, and bows. Taekwon-Do is never used to settle a playground argument.

## Recurring segments (every episode)

1. **Cold open** (first 5-10 s): an action or mystery hook.
2. **Real-world problem:** Kai faces a kid-sized challenge.
3. **The door opens:** practice of a pattern leads into the legend.
4. **The legend:** the namesake's story, told through our kids' eyes.
5. **The Gloom moment:** a tenet beats the Gloom.
6. **Back home:** Kai uses the lesson in real life.
7. **Bori's Fact** (5 s): one true number or fact about the pattern or belt.
8. **Sabum Han's Safety Lesson:** the self-defense segment (see `self-defense-lessons`).
9. **Sign-off:** "Train with heart. See you behind the next door!" plus the bow.
