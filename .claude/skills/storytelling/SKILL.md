# Storytelling for 6-10 year olds

## What this audience loves (design for it)

- **Momentum.** Something happens in the first 5 seconds. A scene longer than ~40 seconds without a change feels slow.
- **A hero just a little older and braver than them.** Kai is 8; he messes up and fixes it.
- **Humor:** physical comedy, silly sidekick, surprise, gentle gross-out (Bori sneezes fire). Not mean jokes.
- **Power and growth:** levelling up, collecting (doors opened, belts earned, Belt Tree growing).
- **Cool facts and numbers** they can repeat at school.
- **Clear good vs. not-good**, but the "bad" is usually a feeling (fear, anger, quitting) that the Gloom feeds on.
- **Repetition and catchphrases.** Ritual segments make the show feel like theirs.

Avoid: long talky scenes, lectures, romance, scary imagery beyond "spooky fog", characters being humiliated, real violence solving problems.

## The lesson rule

One episode = one tenet + one belt/pattern meaning + one self-defense skill. The story *shows* the lesson through a choice the hero makes. Sabum Han may say it in one line at the end; nobody lectures.

## Beat sheet: main episode (3-6 min)

| Time | Beat |
|------|------|
| 0:00-0:08 | Cold open hook (action, mystery, or a laugh) |
| 0:08-0:15 | Title sting |
| 0:15-0:50 | Real-world problem and Kai's mistake |
| 0:50-1:10 | Dojang practice; the door glows |
| 1:10-2:40 | The legend: meet the namesake, a challenge, a choice |
| 2:40-3:10 | The Gloom moment: tenet wins |
| 3:10-3:40 | Back home: Kai fixes his problem |
| 3:40-3:50 | Bori's Fact |
| 3:50-4:40 | Sabum Han's Safety Lesson |
| 4:40-4:50 | Sign-off and bow |

## Beat sheet: short (30-60 s, vertical)

Pick one shape:
- **Legend in 45:** hook question ("Why is a yellow belt yellow?"), 3 fast beats, the answer, a call to try.
- **Safety Lesson short:** problem, Sabum Han shows the move, kids practise, the rule as a chant.
- **Bori's Fact:** one surprising fact with a visual gag.
- **Pattern challenge:** "Can you do the first 3 moves of Chon-Ji?" with slow-motion demonstration.

## Season arc

Each season follows the belt journey (see `itf-lore` season map). Season finale = grading day: Kai earns the next belt and the Belt Tree grows. Plant one mystery per season (who built the Great Clock? why does Sabum Han know about it?) and pay it off slowly so kids come back.

## Checks before handing to script-writing

- Can a 7-year-old explain the lesson in one sentence after watching?
- Is there a laugh at least every 30 seconds?
- Is the lore accurate per `references/itf/`?
- Does Kai make the winning choice himself (not an adult saving him)?
- Does nothing in the story encourage hitting someone over a disagreement?
