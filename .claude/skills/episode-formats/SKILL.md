# Episode formats and platform specs

Verify specs against each platform's current help pages before a season starts; they change. Record the date you checked in `brand/platform-specs-checked.md`.

## Deliverables per episode

| Deliverable | Length | Aspect | Resolution | Notes |
|-------------|--------|--------|------------|-------|
| YouTube main episode | 3-6 min (grow to 8-11 once retention is proven) | 16:9 | 1920x1080 min, 3840x2160 ideal | Chapters in description; end screen last 5-20 s |
| YouTube Short | 30-60 s (platform allows longer; keep short for this age) | 9:16 | 1080x1920 | Hook in first 2 s; loopable ending |
| TikTok | 30-60 s | 9:16 | 1080x1920 | Burned-in captions; keep text out of bottom 20% and right 15% |
| Instagram Reel | 30-60 s | 9:16 | 1080x1920 | Cover frame chosen; same safe zones as TikTok |
| Thumbnail (YouTube) | n/a | 16:9 | 1280x720, under 2 MB | See `thumbnails-and-seo` |

Frame rate: 24 fps for the anime look (keep consistent across a season). Audio: 48 kHz, loudness around -14 LUFS integrated for web, true peak under -1 dBTP.

## Who each platform is for

- **YouTube (main home):** the kids' channel. Every video set to "Made for Kids" (see `child-safety-compliance`). Kids watch here, often via YouTube Kids.
- **TikTok and Instagram:** their users must be 13+, so these accounts are run by the adult owner and **speak to parents and families**: behind-the-scenes, pattern facts, safety lesson clips "to do with your kids", trailers pointing to YouTube. Captions and calls to action address parents. Never encourage children to make accounts or message the account.

## Safe zones (vertical)

Keep faces, text, and key action in the centre 1080x1420 area. Avoid the top ~220 px (status/UI) and bottom ~480 px (captions, buttons). Right edge ~140 px is covered by buttons on TikTok/Reels.

## Captions

- Every deliverable has captions. YouTube: upload an .srt (more accurate than auto). Vertical: burned-in, large, high-contrast, 2 lines max, 3-5 words per line, using the brand font (see `brand-management`).
- Korean terms in captions appear with the romanisation used in `references/itf/terminology.md`.

## Cutting shorts from a main episode

From each main episode, plan at least three verticals:
1. **The legend in 45 s** (the namesake scene plus Bori's Fact).
2. **The safety lesson** (often the best performer with parents).
3. **The funniest moment** with a "full episode on YouTube" end card.

Re-frame, don't just crop: generate or re-composite wide shots for 9:16 where the subject would be cut off. Add a fresh hook line at the start; a short needs its own opening, not the middle of a scene.

## Episode length growth plan

Start short (3-4 min) while production quality and cost are being dialled in. Increase length only when average view duration stays above ~50% for three episodes in a row.
