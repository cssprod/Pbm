# Korean Terminology Glossary (ITF usage)

ITF uses its own romanization (from General Choi's books), which differs from modern official Korean romanization. Use the **ITF spelling** on screen for martial arts terms (that's what kids will hear in class), and the modern spelling for place and people names where helpful. The pronunciation guides are approximate, for voice actors.

## ITF scientific ideas (fun explainer segments)

- **Theory of Power** (General Choi): power comes from **reaction force, concentration, equilibrium (balance), breath control, mass and speed**. Kid version: "Push and pull, focus, balance, breathe, use your whole body, and be fast!" Great for a "science of the kick" mini-segment.
- **Sine wave**: ITF movements rise and fall in a smooth wave, using the body's weight. Kid visual: a wave rolling across the ocean.
- **Kihap**: tightening the body and focusing breath at the moment of impact.
