# Belt Colours and Ranks (Ti 띠)

## The big idea: a belt is the story of a growing tree 🌱 → 🌳

The ITF colour belts tell **one continuous story: a seed growing into a tall tree, reaching for the sky.** This makes a perfect season arc for the series: the main character can "grow" through the belts the way the plant grows.

## Official colour meanings (from General Choi's *Encyclopedia*)

| Colour | Official ITF meaning (paraphrased closely) | Kid version | Visual motif for animation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **White** | Signifies **innocence**, like the beginning student who has no previous knowledge of Taekwon-Do. | "A blank page. You're ready to learn everything!" | Fresh snow, a blank scroll, a seed resting in the ground. |
| **Yellow** | Signifies the **earth**, from which a plant sprouts and takes root as the Taekwon-Do foundation is being laid. | "Your roots are growing. You're building a strong base." | Golden soil, roots spreading, sunlight on the ground. |
| **Green** | Signifies the **plant's growth** as Taekwon-Do skills begin to develop. | "You've sprouted! Your skills are growing." | A green shoot breaking through, leaves unfurling. |
| **Blue** | Signifies the **heaven**, towards which the plant matures into a towering tree as training progresses. | "You're growing tall and reaching for the sky." | A tall tree against a big blue sky, clouds, birds. |
| **Red** | Signifies **danger**, cautioning the student to exercise control and warning the opponent to stay away. | "You're powerful now, so you must be extra careful and in control." | Red sunset, a glowing ember that must be kept safe, a calm warrior holding back. |
| **Black** | The opposite of white, signifying **maturity and proficiency** in Taekwon-Do. It also indicates the wearer's **imperviousness to darkness and fear**. | "You're not done, you're just ready to really begin. And you're not afraid of the dark." | Night sky full of stars, the full-grown tree standing strong at night. |

> Writer's note: "Black belt means a new beginning" is a common teaching across schools. It fits ITF black belt (1st degree = "first step") well, but it's a school tradition, not a line from the *Encyclopedia*.

## Coloured-belt ranks (gup / kup 급)

Coloured belts count **down** from 10th kup to 1st kup. Many schools add a coloured **tag/stripe** for the half-steps. The table shows the most common ITF arrangement. **Schools vary**, so on screen say "at many schools".

| Rank | Belt | Pattern learned for the next grading | Namesake (short) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10th kup | White | Chon-Ji (19 moves) | Heaven & Earth |
| 9th kup | White, yellow stripe | Chon-Ji / Dan-Gun | |
| 8th kup | Yellow | Dan-Gun (21) | Holy Dan-Gun, founder of Korea |
| 7th kup | Yellow, green stripe | Do-San (24) | Patriot Ahn Chang-Ho |
| 6th kup | Green | Won-Hyo (28) | Monk Won-Hyo |
| 5th kup | Green, blue stripe | Yul-Gok (38) | Philosopher Yi I |
| 4th kup | Blue | Joong-Gun (32) | Patriot An Joong-Gun |
| 3rd kup | Blue, red stripe | Toi-Gye (37) | Scholar Yi Hwang |
| 2nd kup | Red | Hwa-Rang (29) | The Hwa-Rang youth group |
| 1st kup | Red, black stripe | Choong-Moo (30) | Admiral Yi Soon-Sin |

The exact mapping of "which pattern at which belt" varies. A common rule: you perform the pattern **of your current belt** at grading, plus earlier ones. `patterns.json` stores the most common grade for each pattern.

## Black belt degrees (dan 단)

| Degree | Title (ITF) | Patterns | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st Dan | Boosabum (assistant instructor, 1st–3rd) | Kwang-Gae, Po-Eun, Ge-Baek | "Il dan". A 1st dan is often thought of as a "new student" at a higher level. |
| 2nd Dan | Boosabum | Eui-Am, Choong-Jang, Ko-Dang *or* Juche | |
| 3rd Dan | Boosabum | Sam-Il, Yoo-Sin, Choi-Yong | |
| 4th Dan | Sabum (instructor, 4th–6th) | Yon-Gae, Ul-Ji, Moon-Moo | |
| 5th Dan | Sabum | So-San, Se-Jong | |
| 6th Dan | Sabum | Tong-Il | Last of the 24 patterns. |
| 7th–8th Dan | Sahyun (master) | | |
| 9th Dan | Saseong (grandmaster) | | Highest degree. |

The ITF says 9 degrees of black belt reflect the number **3 × 3**: in the Orient, 3 is a highly regarded number, and 9 is the highest of the single-digit numbers. It's a nice trivia beat for kids.

## Other uniform facts (good trivia beats)

- **Dobok (도복)**: the uniform. The ITF dobok is white, the colour of innocence, with black trim on black belts' jackets.
- **Tying the belt.** The ITF belt is wrapped **once** around the waist. Many schools teach this as the idea of *oneness*: one master, one purpose, one result (doing one thing with full focus). This is a common school teaching, not something the *Encyclopedia* states clearly, so present it as "many instructors say".
- The ITF crest shows a **tree**-like design inside a fist-and-globe emblem. *(Verify the crest description against whichever ITF group's crest is used before animating. Crests are trademarks, so do **not** reproduce an organization's logo without permission. Design an original series emblem instead.)*
