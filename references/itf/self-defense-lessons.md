# Self-Defense Lessons for Ages 6-10 (one per episode)

Every short episode ends with a **60–90 second self-defense lesson** taught by the in-show instructor to the kid characters, then repeated slowly as "your turn!" for kids at home.

## Ground rules for every lesson (non-negotiable)

1. **Safety first: the best self-defense is not being there.** Most lessons teach awareness, avoidance, words and getting help. Physical techniques are **escape** techniques (get free, run, tell), never "beat them up".
2. **Never teach strikes to the head, neck, eyes, throat or groin** as practice drills for kids at home. Mention "if you're truly in danger, do whatever you need to get away and get help" only in the stranger lessons, without demonstrating.
3. **Never show kids hitting other kids.** Bullying lessons use words, confident posture, walking away and adults.
4. **On-screen safety line** at the start of each physical lesson: *"Practise with a grown-up, slowly, on a soft floor. Only use this if you really need it."*
5. **The ITF oath link:** close every lesson with "I shall never misuse Taekwon-Do."
6. **Trusted adults.** Always tell kids to tell a trusted adult (parent, teacher, instructor, police officer, store worker with a name badge, a mom with kids).
7. **Parent note** in the description of each video: what the lesson covers and how to practise it at home. Encourage enrolling in a real class for hands-on training.
8. **No fear-mongering.** Calm, confident tone. Strangers are *usually* nice; we learn rules "just in case", like fire drills.
9. Content is general educational guidance, **not professional instruction**. Have each physical lesson reviewed by a qualified instructor before publishing.

## Season 1: Awareness and words (no contact)

### SD-01  Be the Boss of Your Space
- **Pairs with:** Chon-Ji (beginning, standing in your own spot).
- **Goal:** Everyone has a personal "bubble" about an arm's length around them.
- **Steps:** Stretch your arms out and spin slowly: that's your bubble. If someone comes too close and you don't like it, step back and say "**Please give me space.**"
- **Game:** Hula-hoop bubble walk.
- **Catchphrase:** "My bubble, my space!"

### SD-02  The Confident Stance (Ready Stance)
- **Pairs with:** Dan-Gun.
- **Goal:** Look strong and calm so bullies pick someone else.
- **Steps:** Feet shoulder-width (like narani junbi sogi), stand tall, shoulders back, eyes up, breathe slow.
- **Game:** "Shrimp vs Mountain": slouch like a shrimp, then stand like a mountain. Which looks braver?
- **Catchphrase:** "Stand tall, look strong, stay calm."

### SD-03  The Power Voice (Kihap for safety)
- **Pairs with:** any pattern (the kihap).
- **Goal:** A loud, strong voice gets attention and help.
- **Steps:** Big breath into your tummy, hand up like a stop sign, shout "**STOP! BACK OFF!**" or "**NO!**". Then get away and tell a grown-up.
- **Game:** Whisper / normal / POWER voice contest.
- **Safety line:** Use the power voice when you need help, not to scare your little sister!

### SD-04  The Stop Hand (the fence)
- **Pairs with:** Do-San.
- **Goal:** Hands up and open between you and a person, palms out, means "stop".
- **Steps:** Step back with one foot, both hands up in front of your chest, palms forward, say "Stop, I don't want to." Hands up looks calm but keeps people out of your bubble.
- **Catchphrase:** "Step back, hands up, power voice."

### SD-05  No, Go, Yell, Tell
- **Pairs with:** Won-Hyo (wisdom, using your mind).
- **Goal:** The four-step safety rule for scary or tricky situations.
- **Steps:** Say **NO**, **GO** (get away), **YELL** (power voice), **TELL** a trusted adult.
- **Game:** "What would you do?" scenarios with a puppet (an adult offering puppies or candy, a stranger asking for help finding a lost dog). Grown-ups ask grown-ups for help, not kids.
- **Catchphrase:** "No, Go, Yell, Tell!"

### SD-06  My Trusted Adults Team
- **Pairs with:** Yul-Gok (the mother who teaches).
- **Goal:** Know 5 grown-ups you can tell anything, and know your parent's phone number by heart.
- **Steps:** Trace your hand; write one trusted adult on each finger. Practise your parent's phone number as a song.
- **Catchphrase:** "Five fingers, five helpers."

### SD-07  If You Get Lost
- **Pairs with:** Kwang-Gae (finding your way home).
- **Goal:** Stay put, find a safe helper.
- **Steps:** Stop and stay where you are (be a tree). Look for a safe helper: a worker with a name badge at a checkout, a police officer, a mom with kids. Say your name and your grown-up's name and phone number.
- **Catchphrase:** "Stop, stay, find a helper."

### SD-08  Walk Away Like a Black Belt (bullying 1)
- **Pairs with:** Toi-Gye or Ul-Ji (win without fighting).
- **Goal:** Walking away is not losing. It's self-control.
- **Steps:** Stay calm, say something short and strong ("That's not cool" or "Whatever, I'm going"), walk away toward adults or friends, and tell a grown-up.
- **Catchphrase:** "The strongest move is walking away."

### SD-09  Words That Shield (bullying 2)
- **Pairs with:** Po-Eun (the poem battle).
- **Goal:** Use calm, confident words and humour instead of fists.
- **Steps:** Agree and shrug ("Yeah, maybe, anyway..."), say "Stop" with your power voice, and get help. Practise "I" statements: "I don't like it when you..."
- **Catchphrase:** "My words are my shield."

### SD-10  Stand Up for a Friend (upstander, not bystander)
- **Pairs with:** Hwa-Rang (be a true friend).
- **Goal:** Help a friend being picked on, safely.
- **Steps:** Go stand next to your friend, say "Come on, let's go," walk away together, tell a grown-up. *Never* fight the bully.
- **Catchphrase:** "Together we're stronger."

### SD-11  Online Safety Shield
- **Pairs with:** Se-Jong (letters and messages).
- **Goal:** Never share personal information online; tell a grown-up if something online feels weird.
- **Steps:** Don't share your name, school, address or photos. Never meet someone from online. If something makes you feel funny inside: close it and tell a grown-up.
- **Catchphrase:** "Private stays private."

### SD-12  Body Safety: My Body Belongs to Me
- **Pairs with:** Yoo-Sin (integrity).
- **Goal:** Kids are the boss of their body; no secrets about touching; tell a trusted adult.
- **Steps:** "No" is okay, even with people you know. Surprises are okay (they end in a happy reveal), but secrets that make you feel bad are not. Tell, and keep telling until someone listens.
- **Note:** Keep this age-appropriate and simple; follow established body-safety curricula wording (e.g. "safe and unsafe touch"). Consider a parent note with links to child-safety organizations.

## Season 2: Escapes and safe movement (light contact, with a grown-up)

### SD-13  Falling Safely (breakfalls)
- **Pairs with:** Choong-Moo (getting back up).
- **Goal:** Fall without getting hurt.
- **Steps:** Tuck your chin (look at your belt), round your back like a rocking chair, slap the mat with your arms. Start sitting down, then from squatting.
- **Safety line:** Only on a soft mat or grass.
- **Catchphrase:** "Chin tucked, roll like a ball."

### SD-14  Wrist Grab Escape (same-side)
- **Pairs with:** Joong-Gun (strong hands, peaceful heart).
- **Goal:** Get free if someone grabs your wrist.
- **Steps:** Make a fist, power voice "LET GO!", then pull or twist your arm **toward their thumb** (the weakest part of the grip), bending your elbow toward them. Then run to a safe adult.
- **Game:** Grown-up holds gently; the kid finds "the thumb door".
- **Catchphrase:** "Find the thumb door!"

### SD-15  Two-Hand Wrist Grab Escape
- **Pairs with:** Ge-Baek (teamwork: both hands together).
- **Steps:** Reach your free hand through, grab your own fist, and pull up and out through the thumbs. Power voice, run, tell.

### SD-16  Sleeve or Shirt Grab Escape
- **Pairs with:** Choong-Jang.
- **Steps:** Step back, big circle with your arm over the top of their arm (like the windmill), and step away. Power voice, run, tell.

### SD-17  Don't Get Dragged: The Heavy Turtle
- **Pairs with:** Choong-Moo (turtle ship).
- **Goal:** If someone tries to pull you somewhere, make yourself heavy and loud.
- **Steps:** Drop your weight low (sitting stance / annun sogi or even sit down), power voice "**HELP! THIS IS NOT MY DAD/MOM!**", grab onto something (pole, bike rack).
- **Catchphrase:** "Low, heavy, loud!"

### SD-18  Bear-Hug Escape (from behind)
- **Pairs with:** Dan-Gun (the bear!).
- **Goal:** Get free if grabbed from behind, without needing to be big.
- **Steps:** Drop your weight (bend knees), elbows out wide, wiggle and turn, power voice, run to an adult. *Demonstrate slowly with an adult; no strikes.*
- **Safety line:** Practise slowly with a grown-up only.

### SD-19  Pushed? Stay Up (balance)
- **Pairs with:** Ko-Dang (strong without punching).
- **Goal:** A strong stance helps you stay balanced if pushed in a line or on the playground.
- **Steps:** Walking stance (gunnun sogi), knees bent, eyes up. Step back and walk away; tell a grown-up.

### SD-20  Safe From Dogs: Be a Tree
- **Pairs with:** So-San (calm like a monk).
- **Goal:** Stay safe around unknown dogs.
- **Steps:** Stop, stand still like a tree (feet together, hands folded, look at your feet), count calmly. Never run from a dog. Always ask the owner before petting. If knocked down: be a rock (curl up, hands over your neck).
- **Catchphrase:** "Be a tree."

## Season 3: Smart choices (situational awareness)

### SD-21  Spot the Exits
- **Pairs with:** Yon-Gae (the fortress).
- **Goal:** When you walk into a place, notice the doors, the helpers and where your grown-up is.
- **Game:** "Exit detective" at the supermarket.

### SD-22  The Buddy System
- **Pairs with:** Sam-Il (people standing together).
- **Goal:** Always go places with a buddy (playground, bathrooms at events, walking home).
- **Catchphrase:** "Two is safer than one."

### SD-23  Tricky People (not just strangers)
- **Pairs with:** Choi-Yong (gold is just a stone).
- **Goal:** "Tricky people" ask kids for help, offer gifts or ask kids to keep secrets. Safe grown-ups don't ask kids for help, and won't be upset if you check with your grown-up first.
- **Steps:** "I have to ask my mom/dad first." Then go.
- **Catchphrase:** "Check first!"

### SD-24  Calm Body, Clear Mind (breathing when scared)
- **Pairs with:** Won-Hyo, Moon-Moo (calming the waves).
- **Goal:** Box breathing to calm fear or anger before deciding what to do.
- **Steps:** Breathe in 4, hold 4, out 4, hold 4. "Flute breathing" (the Dragon King's flute) as the show's version.
- **Catchphrase:** "Calm the waves."

### SD-25  Making Peace (conflict resolution)
- **Pairs with:** Tong-Il (two becoming one).
- **Goal:** Solve arguments with friends: cool down, take turns talking, find a fair deal, say sorry.
- **Catchphrase:** "Cool down, talk it out, shake it out."
